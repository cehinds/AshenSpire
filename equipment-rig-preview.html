Select a key pose to compare its silhouette and inspect the joints. Intermediate clearance poses keep the limbs from folding through the shoulder.

Joint limits and foot contacts

Elbows: 8–165° flexion. Knees: 8–145°. Wrists: ±50°. Ankles: ±60°. Spine: −12–40°. Shoulder and hip rotation also have bounded ranges. These are animation authoring limits. Green foot markers show ground contact; amber marks a lifted step.

Adding a weapon

Artwork + grip coordinates + weapon family. The katana and straight sword use identical motion data. No new body frames.

What this proves

Bounded joints, moving legs, weight shifts and lifted steps across attacks, casts and guards. Enable joints and scrub to inspect hands and foot contact.

Prototype scope

Two base outfits combine painted torso variants with moving arms, hips and legs. This preview adapts the pose silhouettes; it is not a pixel-for-pixel recreation or a live combat replacement.
