# Lore

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `LORE.md` | The lore bible: premise, the three Seats and the fourth, why the cities died, cinders and the Corrupted, the Forsaken, the four who climb, card-flavour voice, author vocabulary |
| `LORE-CAST.md` | Climbers' motives, companions, quest givers, enemies and what they were, consequences before and after the fall |
| `LORE-WORLD.md` | Ages, magic, war, faiths, materials, peoples, the Cinderwrights, the food chain, factions |
| `LORE-EMBER.md` | What the Sovereign Ember may be, and the rules that keep it unsettled |
| `in-game-text.json` | Every player-facing prose string extracted from the shipped content, keyed by the original ids: card names and flavour, relics, events and choices, speakers, quests and surveys, classes, armaments and armour blurbs, keepsakes, world-atlas places, legacy dungeons |

`in-game-text.json` holds prose only — names, flavour, descriptions, dialogue
and result text. Costs, numbers, effects and rules were deliberately dropped.
