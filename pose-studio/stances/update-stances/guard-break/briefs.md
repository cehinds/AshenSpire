# Reaver greatsword guard-break pose exploration

All options show the same Reaver in battered silver and bronze armor with a continuous crimson cape and a full-size greatsword. The camera stays behind the left shoulder in a rear three-quarter view, looking toward screen-right. Each pose is intended to show exactly one knee grounded and the opposite boot planted, with exhausted involuntary collapse rather than a deliberate kneel.

## Sheet A

| ID | Pose brief | Review |
|---|---|---|
| A01 | **Forward-Folded Sword Support.** The Reaver folds low over the planted greatsword, with uneven shoulders, drooping head, and hands lowered to the hilt. | Accepted after targeted revision. |
| A02 | **Ground Drag + Thigh Catch.** One gauntlet weakly holds the sword low at the side while the free hand catches the raised thigh; the body leans toward the weapon. | Accepted. |
| A03 | **Palm Catch + Rear Drag.** One palm catches the fall ahead while the other hand drags the greatsword behind; the opposite boot remains planted. | Accepted. |
| A04 | **Sideways Open Guard.** The body buckles sideways with one arm hanging open and the sword held low outward. | Flagged: blade overlaps A05's rear boot and its length is inconsistent. |
| A05 | **Dropped Greatsword + Slack Arms.** The weapon has slipped to the floor beyond the planted boot; both arms hang slack as the Reaver folds sideways. | Accepted. |
