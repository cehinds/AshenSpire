# Greatsword smash: first exploration

These are ten alternative HELD loading stances, not animation frames or runtime-ready sprites. Reaver is the first weapon/class pilot. Same rear-left camera, facing right, costume, sword, two hands on hilt, and continuous crimson cape in every option. No effects.

Observed project references: `assets-display/alternative/stances/reaver/offensive.webp` and `pose-studio/stances/source/reaver-default.png`, both inspected with view_image. The ordinary attack already uses an overhead blade; smash should communicate greater compression, coiling and planted force.

The external reference researcher reported image pixels inaccessible through the connector and network download as of initial preparation. External mechanic descriptions are art-direction inference, not claimed direct visual observation. See `docs/design/update-stances/references.md` for the research record when available.

## Sheet A: row-major 1–5; bottom-right cell empty

1. **Deep high-guard squat.** Hips dropped very low, wide flat-foot base, elbows bent and compressed beside helmet, blade nearly vertical above rear shoulder. The loaded body distinguishes it from ordinary overhead attack.
2. **Long rear-loaded diagonal.** Long forward foot brace, rear knee deeply bent, torso pitched slightly back, both hands at rear shoulder, blade extending diagonally back/down-left. Grip remains at shoulder height.
3. **Cross-body waist coil.** Wide side-on squat, hips twisted away while helmet targets right, hands at rear waist, blade nearly horizontal back-left. Strong transverse shoulder/hip opposition.
4. **Low near-ground rising load.** Compact forward crouch, front knee deep, hands low near front thigh, blade extended back-left close to ground, poised to drive a rising smash.
5. **Behind-head sledge load.** Elbows folded beside and behind crown, sword nearly horizontal high back-left, torso slightly arched and knees compressed, preparing a downward hammer strike.

## Sheet B: row-major 6–10; bottom-right cell empty

6. **High unilateral weight shift.** Weight on deeply bent rear leg, forward foot light on toe, torso tall but coiled, two hands high, sword diagonal up-right. Distinct raised threat and asymmetric weight balance.
7. **Broad lateral shoulder load.** Extra-wide lateral lunge, front leg nearly straight and rear knee low, hands tightly beside rear shoulder, blade horizontal at shoulder level back-left.
8. **Compact chest-high crushing load.** Narrow staggered stance, elbows tight against ribs, both hands at chest height across body, blade tipped up-left, shoulders rounded into a compact load.
9. **Heel-lift rotational coil.** Long stagger, rear heel raised, visibly rotated hips and hunched torso, accessible two-hand grip beside rear shoulder, sword diagonal across upper back. No impossible hidden behind-back grip.
10. **Upright rear-hip pendulum.** Wide rooted base, torso upright and braced, both hands at rear hip, sword downward/back diagonal with tip above ground; loaded heavy upswing. Upright posture distinguishes it from option 4.
