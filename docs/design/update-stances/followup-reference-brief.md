## Silhouette and held-state review at 32 px

For each future candidate, review both its full-resolution anatomy and a **32px-high character silhouette** (including full sword; scale every character body consistently). Test on a neutral contrasting backing as well as in the comparison gallery; transparent image hidden RGB is not evidence of a background defect.

1. **Label-free family read:** Sweep implies lateral/rising arc; Counter presents an interception plane with return path; Evade changes body position while protecting a recoverable weapon carry. Do not ask for supernatural effects to establish the family.
2. **Grounding:** two separate boot contacts, a support base beneath the projected center of mass, no floating sole or raised-foot hold. Counter06's transient stepping read is specifically excluded from new holds.
3. **Pairwise distinction:** compare every new option against its family's selected04/06/08 and all nine new peers. Require at least **two visible changes** among body height/lean, support width/weight side, hilt height, blade direction, and elbow space. A cape variation or tint counts as zero.
4. **Weapon legibility:** sword remains unmistakably a long greatsword; hilt connects to blade and both hands; point and pommel survive crop. Hands may simplify at32px, but the full-resolution grip must prove correctness.
5. **Identity/camera:** closed helmet looks right, rear-left armor and continuous cape remain readable; no front breastplate portrait or copied reference outfit. Do not turn the actor around to obtain variety.
6. **Hold versus motion:** imagine a one-second quiet freeze, then a believable transition back to neutral. Raised boots, exaggerated cape trails, extreme spinal arcs, contact recoil, or full-extension impact reads belong to action frames, not these holds.
