## Evidence status and limits

The connected web search was used for real people, historical drawings, manga/manhwa, comics, and games. Source pages and direct image URLs are recorded below. **No external source image can honestly be classified as directly visually inspected in this research session.** Image search supplied descriptive captions; successful image opens and PDF screenshot requests returned `ImageDisplayed` text markers, but no image pixels/ImageContent to inspect. Shell retrieval of source images failed with proxy `403 Forbidden`. The World Archery Level 2 PDF also exceeded the web tool's 10 MB limit. Consequently:

- **T:** source page text read through the web tool.
- **D:** image-search description only; useful discovery lead, not an observed image.
- **O:** direct image/PDF screenshot request succeeded, but pixels were not delivered; still not an observed image.
- **V:** direct visual inspection. No external reference has this status yet.

Do not turn the captions into a claim of verified anatomy, exact camera angle, or sequential motion. Any reference-camera compatibility below is provisional. The 60 concepts and weapon mappings are **original art-direction inferences**, not copies or claims that a source demonstrates that precise pose. External visual verification remains outstanding because of the image-delivery limitation; this is an evidence limitation, not a task assigned to the user. Official/publisher sources are preferred when available; discovery mirrors are explicitly identified. No reference artwork is imported into the game.

The latest requested count is **ten concepts per weapon per family**: ten weapon sets × six families × ten concepts = **600 weapon-specific concepts before class adaptations**. The 60 rows below are preliminary shared motion vocabulary and do not satisfy that full count. Weapon-specific expansion must deliberately differentiate grips, tool paths, balance, and silhouette; repeating one generic row with ten weapon labels does not complete the brief.

## Weapon handling drives the pose

These are production constraints and source-informed inferences, not equipment additions. Only draw a listed set where the character actually equips it. A class costume adaptation should retain the same pose grammar, then conform the grip and tools to that actor's established equipment. If an actor lacks that weapon combination, leave the combination unsupported rather than silently inventing it.

| Weapon set | Attack and held readiness | Defend and counter | Smash / break constraints | Main research leads |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sword + shield | Shield left-forward; sword right on an independent line; point/hilt visible beyond torso. | Cover body with shield plane, reply around its upper or lower edge; do not hide sword inside shield. | Shield can drop/tilt outward during collapse; sword loses its threatening line. Heavy blow is one-handed unless shield is absent. | R05, R19, R24 |
| Greatsword | Two separated hands on one hilt; bent elbows and shoulder load for anticipation; broad weapon path. | High roof, crossed line, or angled hanging blade; hands remain on the hilt. | Compression must precede impact; weapon's mass changes the hips and feet. Break may use blade tip as incidental support, not a prayer emblem. | R03, R08–R13, R21 |
| Staff | Clearly spaced hands for two-hand manipulation; for a caster's one-hand staff, preserve that class setup. | Two-handed redirect or one-hand staff with separate casting palm/book gesture. | Do not treat light shaft as a slab sword; make the direction obvious from pole angle. | R18, R26, R28 |
| Bow | Bow hand forward, string hand near face at full draw; actual string/arrow relationship. | Reposition lower body while upper shooting line stabilizes; full-draw reply reads better than decorative bow waving. | Break relaxes the draw; anticipate with partial draw, attack at release/follow-through. Do not make an arrowless drawn string. | R14–R17 |
| Single dagger | One short blade, free hand distinct and close enough to help intercept. | Hand/forearm cover and short reply; require a plausible contact direction without opponent clutter. | Compact load, not oversized sword reach; break allows free hand to brace. | R06, R07, R10 |
| Dual daggers | Two visible blades, stagger their heights and functions; separated wrists. | Lead blade intercepts, rear blade answers; symmetric crossed X is only one option. | Short paths and compact torso; do not fuse hilts or mirror hands into duplicate anatomy. | R06, R22, R23 |
| Single sword | One-hand point and free-hand balance; guard shoulder line keeps orientation right. | Small redirect and extension, high/low cover, off-line footwork. | Avoid applying two-hand greatsword load to a one-hand sword. | R04, R05, R07 |
| Spear | Point aimed along target line; front hand guides, rear hand drives, visibly separated. | Beat/thrust, retract and answer, butt-end cover where plausible. | Distinguish a long thrust from a halberd chop; keep long shaft and both hands inside crop. | R18, R28 |
| Halberd | Wider hand separation and heavier head-driven diagonal chop; readable blade/head orientation. | Hook/redirect, hanging cover, butt-end reply, sliding grip only if anatomically clear. | Shaft follows both hands; blade mass affects body load. Avoid spinning decorative pole parallel to body. | R18 |
| Unarmed | Guard and free-hand balance; hip-led punch/palm rather than a weapon mime. | One hand intercepts while the other replies; shoulders/hips indicate force and line. | Break may brace with a palm; heavy smash can be a grounded double-palm or elbow drive, preserving the actor's identity. | R06, R20, R29 |

Additional held families: **Attack** keeps credible ready distance and a weapon line; **Defend** makes an interception plane readable; **Prepare** stores energy without already contacting; **Magic variants** distinguish gather, protect, redirect, and release through hand spacing and pole/book angle. Never use effects alone to rename an unchanged pose.

## Preliminary motion vocabulary: ten shared ideas in each of six families

Each row is an original pose proposition for the fixed rear-left/right-facing camera. Apply the weapon rules above before class costume. At thumbnail size the planted foot, grounded knee, torso pitch, shoulders, hands, and weapon line should differ—not merely effects or cloak shape. Source IDs indicate inspiration leads, not an observed exact match.

### Guard break — loss of structure, still alive

| ID | Distinct silhouette / motion idea | Body and equipment cue | Leads |

| GB03 | Side-collapse with bracing palm | Left palm or shield rim low outside left hip, right knee down; torso tilted but back visible. | R01, R06 |
