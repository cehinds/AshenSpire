## Known remaining work

- Iron Guard needs a final accepted board-matched painting. The candidate stays clearly labelled in the gallery and manifest.
- Fine brushwork, faces and miniature details are reconstructed, so they will differ from the original pixels. Review the side-by-side source details before selecting them for runtime integration.
- The prior package's scenes and enemy perspectives have not all been re-certified by this correction. Keep its supporting backgrounds available, but do not infer acceptance from a matching filename.
- Board-specific full-body class/foreground poses, armour and jewellery, additional relic/flask objects and the compendium still-life remain outside this correction. The source-board audit lists these explicitly; no generic substitute is newly labelled faithful here.
- Complete assembled desktop and mobile game screens, long localized copy, actual control states and physical-device interaction require runtime implementation and review. The standalone gallery is a component-review aid.

`review/` images are static composition checks. The local interactive preview was blocked by automatic app URL approval policy, so browser or physical-device acceptance is not claimed.
