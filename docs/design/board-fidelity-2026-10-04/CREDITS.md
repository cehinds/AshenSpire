# Source and rights

New paintings and cutouts are original OpenAI image-generator artwork directed for AshenSpire, using the project's original concept boards as visual references. The exact prompts and returned-file hashes are in `provenance/`. No third-party artwork was downloaded and no third-party license is claimed.

Native SVG controls, card geometry and icons are project-authored editable artwork based on the project's concept boards. The original boards are copied unchanged from `docs/design/player-polish-2026-10-01/`. The weathered parchment material is an unchanged copy from the previous original project-generated component collection.

Cinzel, Cormorant Garamond and Inter font files are unchanged copies from the previous kit. Their SIL Open Font License is retained in `fonts/OFL.txt`.

This is reference/authoring material. Runtime promotion follows the repository's external-art release workflow; this package itself does not publish or replace a runtime art release.
