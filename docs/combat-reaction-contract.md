# Combat reactions, actor reveal and player overhead HUD

Owner request, 2026-10-08. This extends SPEC sections 4.8, 4.9 and 7.4. The contract must land in its own PR before runtime implementation.

## Actor order and Counter presentation

- Enemies act one at a time in the existing roster order. An actor's start, direct contacts, eligible return, remaining effects and owner-turn completion precede the next actor.
- A successful armed Counter retains the current coverage, full-resistance, charge, damage, control and lifetime rules. Its return has a separate actor animation and impact beat. Playback pauses the attacking actor while the returning actor animates and applies its damage, then resumes remaining legal work. No cosmetic timer enters the headless engine.
- Multi-hit roots retain one Counter decision after all direct contacts, status pressure and impact. Their return never moves ahead of the contacts required to establish success. A defeated or interrupted actor cannot perform a cancelled continuation.
- Reduced motion, instant playback and explicit animation skip reach the identical committed state. Skipping presentation never answers a pending gameplay choice.

## Optional cards during an incoming action

- Versioned reaction rules enable a headless, resumable choice before an incoming hostile attack or Counter return resolves against a living connected player. Merely preparing a Counter stance does not immediately play a Sweep.
- Eligible options are owned Counter cards matching the incoming coverage, and Sweep cards explicitly authored to allow a defensive reaction. Ordinary offensive Sweeps retain their normal player-turn use. Defensive eligibility is data, not a card-name branch.
- The card must still be in that player's hand, playable under current control and card rules, and affordable using the normal Stamina, Mana, HP and other payment checks. Any choice and Upcast tier are validated normally. Options display actual costs; selection spends nothing.
- The end-turn hand and remaining Stamina remain available until enemy-phase reaction opportunities finish. Commit explicit turn-end discards and their replacement-draw receipts before offering any reaction; a discarded instance is never eligible. End-turn hooks and status decay run once; remaining unplayed discard/Exhaust/Retain and unused-Stamina cleanup run once after reactions, before the next draw and refill.
- A modal offers every currently eligible option and a footer ordered **Back, Play**. Back skips this offered reaction and resumes the incoming action; it does not return to card browsing. Header close and Escape have the same behavior. The modal owns focus and freezes ordinary hand, turn, movement and menu actions. Only the targeted player may answer in co-op; other seats observe the pause. Back, Escape and previews spend nothing and do not reroll the incoming action.
- Playing one selected card pays once, removes that exact instance once, and resolves its printed support/attack effects before the paused action. Counter prepares defense and uses the existing successful-return rules; it does not deal its return damage merely because it was played. A defensive Sweep resolves its printed effects. An incoming action is cancelled only when its normal death/interruption rules require cancellation.
- Each incoming root or automatic return offers at most one defensive card per affected seat. The selected reaction and any return it produces cannot recursively offer another reaction choice. Existing automatic Counter anti-chain and per-cycle limits remain in force. A Counter explicitly selected and paid from this prompt may defend against an automatic return and produce one bounded return if the ordinary coverage/full-resistance/control rules and per-cycle limit permit it. This paid manual reply terminates the chain: no further automatic Counter or reaction prompt is allowed. Merely holding an armed Counter does not create automatic Counter-versus-Counter chains.
- Collect affected-seat choices in the existing saved seat order before beginning the incoming defense receipt, consuming Counter charges or rolling avoidance. Preserve already committed incoming contact plans. Give each offer a stable identity; stale/duplicate/foreign answers are rejected without mutation. A disconnected or defeated owner declines deterministically; if no connected living seat remains, suspend the saved continuation until reconnect. Rejoin adopts the existing cursor and cannot reopen a settled offer.
- Keep reaction-choice ancestry separate from the existing `combatReaction` carrier flag, which changes combat damage/Counter rules. Resume the incoming foundation root serial, rolls and trigger budgets exactly; the accepted reaction card gets its own bounded action context without resetting the paused root.
- Pending choice, incoming action receipt, remaining queue, enemy cursor, hand cleanup state, paid reaction state and RNG counters persist exactly. Reload resumes the choice or continuation without repeating hooks, payment, damage, learning or rolls. Rejected choices are atomic and inert. Co-op authority validates ownership and durably accepts the candidate before broadcasting/adopting it.
- New eligible expanded runs snapshot reaction rules version 1. Historical runs/snapshots lacking those rules retain their carried mechanical turn lifecycle. Presentation and HUD fixes apply without repricing historical combat.

## Intent reveal

- At the start of each living enemy's own turn, reveal that actor's actual committed intent before its animation or any reaction prompt. Unstarted enemies retain their existing hidden/read projection.
- Public revelation does not change the original private read, prediction eligibility or learning receipt. It grants no Perception XP on its own. Record performed moves and mark predictions executed only when the surviving, non-cancelled payload actually starts after reaction choices. A Sweep that defeats or interrupts the attacker before execution grants no correct-prediction credit for that cancelled action.
- Refresh next-action intents at player-turn start. Delayed actions retain their committed identity and serial until resolution; charging and ready spell labels follow the existing contract. Actor revelation is reset for the next player planning phase without additional visibility draws for an unchanged delayed action.
- Solo and co-op project the same public executing action. Tooltip, inspector, overhead and animation agree; no future unstarted action leaks through the reveal. An automatic Counter return projects its own committed carrier, never the returning enemy's unrelated selected future move.

## Reaction switch and combat log

- Place Combat log on the right side of the battlefield, with a small Reaction switch directly below. These controls remain clear of the hand, footer and menu buttons on desktop and phone layouts.
- Optional reaction prompts start enabled for each eligible player in a new combat. The switch changes only that player's future optional prompts; an armed Counter continues to resolve normally. Store this combat-scoped preference in the combat snapshot. Co-op accepts a switch command only for its connected owner through the ordinary durable command boundary. Switching and reading the log spend no resources and make no random draws. Historical combat retains its saved mechanical lifecycle.
- Show **Reaction enabled** or **Reaction disabled**, using green or red fill respectively, white text with a black outline and a gold switch border/thumb matching the existing motif. Expose a named keyboard-operable switch with its checked state; text conveys the state without relying on color.
- Combat log unfolds upward in place beside its button, with a slightly translucent, readable background and a scrollable list of completed actions grouped by numbered round. Opening, scrolling and changing size never change combat state. Public entries describe committed cards, executed enemy moves, Counter returns, damage, defense, statuses and skipped reactions; they must not disclose an unstarted hidden intent or another seat's private read or prediction. Reload and co-op resync retain history without duplicating receipts.
- Tap Combat log to open or close it. Drag its button vertically to resize, snapping to **Small**, **Medium**, or **Large** on release; no size buttons appear inside the log. Arrow Up/Down and Home/End provide keyboard resizing. Small uses about half the card height, Medium half the viewport, and Large the space below the menu. On short screens, distribute three distinct stops within that available height. Resizing and cancellation preserve combat state and scrolling.
- The compact Log/Reaction toolbar sits at the right of the card section, above the resting cards. Selected card inspection remains independently reachable.

## Combatant selection HUD

- Idle enemies show only the projected intent symbol (plus `?` when unread), a slim HP strip above the body, and applicable status symbols. Selection reveals the existing intent text/amounts and the name/HP panel below the body. Unknown projections never invent an action or expose hidden amounts.
- Sprite, intent, and health controls select the same owner; armed legal cards use those same targets. The player's HP strip also confirms self-cast cards. The small Information button remains above the player; on a selected combatant one tap opens its inspector.
- Idle player HP sits below the artwork, raised only enough to clear the toolbar. Selecting the player reveals name, level, Poise, Ward and HP in a dark panel beside the lower body. Keep it clear of enemy sprites, controls and the hand toolbar.
- Selected enemies have red silhouette and Information outlines; selected players use green. Selection temporarily raises only that sprite above other combatants and applies 105% scale around its ground anchor. Deselect restores its original layer and size. Card playback cannot accumulate scale or change the resting anchor; melee motion still returns home.
- A selected card's authored maneuver supplies the player's overhead action icon. Do not infer action identity from card names.
- Header HP occupies the available width before Armoury, leaving padding; fill is current/max. Existing utility icons, card rendering/fan and footer composition are preserved.
- Labels fit their available width with padding. Compact and expanded controls are independently selectable, and reserved intent widths include their attached idle HP strips.

## Required verification

- Headless solo/co-op tests: ordered multi-enemy actions, full/partial absorption, cancelled returns, multi-hit success, affordable/absent/controlled cards, multiple options, selection/Back neutrality, one payment, defensive versus offensive Sweep, incoming Counter, finite reaction depth, ownership and disconnect behavior. Verify owned switch persistence, prompt suppression without disabling armed Counters, and atomic refusal at the durable boundary.
- Exact save/restore at each pause: pending offer, paid nested card choice and continuation; compare entire combat and every RNG stream counter. Verify historical snapshots retain their mechanical lifecycle.
- Browser desktop/native-touch tests: actual attack, separate Counter animation/impact, frozen modal and selection, actor-specific reveal and next-player-turn concealment, keyboard focus, animation skip/reduced motion, and HUD gap before/after selection at multiple UI scales.
- Browser controls tests: Back/Play order and skip behavior, switch label/color/keyboard/touch behavior, round history without private leaks or duplicates, and all three log heights and menu/hand clearance on desktop and phone layouts.
- Review and preserve both regular and alternative presentation. Complete generated receipt/build gates and exact-head regular/alternative dev/test delivery. No release/main promotion.
