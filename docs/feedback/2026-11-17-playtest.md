# Playtest feedback — 2026-11-17

42 items, grouped into 6 themes. Each theme becomes one branch and one PR into `dev`.

## A. Combat math (`chore/BalanceStats`)

1. Base damage halved.
2. AR, DR and PR bonuses reduced. Each now scales from one stat:
   - AR from STR
   - DR from DEX
   - PR from max(INT, WIS)
3. HP = 30 + 5 × (CON + level). (Owner answer, 2026-11-17.)
   - The AR/DR/PR cut comes from each stat now scaling from one source only (item 2); there is no separate multiplier.
4. Armor and armaments no longer grant AR or DR. Instead each gives +1 to +3 stat points (average 1–2) and a poise value.
5. Extra turns and MP each cost 3 stat points per +1.
6. Inflated stats are cut by effective level per act (Acts 1 / 2 / 3):
   - bosses: 4 / 6 / 8
   - elites: 2 / 4 / 6
   - normal enemies: unchanged
7. Upgrade cost starts at about 50 cinders and rises with rank and with the number of earlier upgrades.

## B. Status and stance rules (`chore/StanceStatusRules`)

8. Counter loses 1 at the start of the next turn, following the normal decay rule.
9. Defense stance while block is above 0:
   - The character keeps at least 1 poise, so physical attacks and sweeps cannot break it.
   - Magic and smash still break poise.
   - At 0 block, poise can break normally.
10. Defense stance with block and ward stacks: magic damage also cannot break poise (poise = max(poise − dmg, 1)).
11. Gilded defense/counter shows a counter badge. The badge decays normally and also drops by 1 on each use.

## C. Card design (`chore/CardCostBalance`)

12. Every card that grants evade carries a drawback, for example:
    - costs 2–3 SP, or exhausts if it costs 0–1
    - costs MP
    - is ethereal
    - its cost rises by 1 on each use
    - deals damage to you

    Upgrades ease the drawback or add stacks, tags or effects.
13. Hold Block gets the same kind of drawback, and upgrades ease it in the same way.
14. Unupgraded cards that deal too much damage get a penalty.
15. Star Seer's ethereal starter block stays at 10 block. Its upgrade choices are: reaction, remove ethereal, or another effect.
16. Pebble Throw becomes a reach attack.
17. Cards that buff team members are removed from the pool.
18. Card text and inspect tooltips are rewritten to match the current tags, shorter and plainer.

## D. Deck rules and progression (`chore/DeckProgression`)

19. Customize lets you assign specific cards.
20. New relic **Basic Training**: unlimited copies of basic unranked martial cards.
21. New relic **Graduate**: unlimited copies of cantrips (unranked magic cards).
22. New relics that lower the minimum number of card slots.
23. Level progression also lowers the minimum card slots. With relics and levels together, a player should reach a minimum of 8.
24. The progression screen shows the bonuses the character actually gets, with a class-specific bonus at every odd level.
25. Spending stat points on level-up opens its own modal again (the old level-up modal) instead of an inline panel.

## G. Conditional cards and generator layers (`chore/ConditionalCards`)

46. More self-buff cards. These buff the player only; team-buff cards stay out of the pool.
47. Cards that pay off on a condition, either the enemy's or the player's own:

    - status
    - combat range
48. The procedural card generator gains effect layers for the tags that have none today.
