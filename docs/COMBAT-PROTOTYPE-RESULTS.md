## Interpretation

The standard encounters were too forgiving to measure survival balance: every build won every scored run. Doubling incoming damage exposed long-sequence attrition and the caster fallback after mana was depleted. The shared greedy policy spends mana for immediate value and does not reserve it for later encounters; these results do not isolate card balance from policy quality. Heavy physical leads impact, Bleed leads sustained boss damage, and the caster leads group clear speed. Complete item-derived profiles and route-aware playtesting before expanding reward content.
