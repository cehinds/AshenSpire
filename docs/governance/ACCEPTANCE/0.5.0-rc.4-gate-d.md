## Why this file exists, and what its existence says

This is the **first Gate D record in the repository.** `rc.1`, `rc.2` and `rc.3`
each reached `test`, and `rc.2` went on to `release` (F-11), with no recorded
five-role acceptance for any of them — a search for "Gate D" across `docs/` and
`.agentops/` returns decision 0009, the release runbook, and nothing else.

That is stated first because it is the finding, not a preamble to one. Decision
0009 makes Gate D a precondition for Gate E and Gate E a precondition for
Gate F, so a release cut that happened without a recorded D did not merely skip
a form: it skipped the step where independent seats get to say no. Opening the
record at `rc.4` does not backfill `rc.1`–`rc.3`, and nothing here should be
read as doing so.
