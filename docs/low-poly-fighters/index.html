Low-Poly Fighters — Painted Poses

Painted orthographic reference sheets for the four AshenSpire classes: every equipment piece shown in five views, dark steel and leather with gold trim over a low-poly faceted surface. Two sheets per class — the garments and the kit (hands, feet, weapon). The in-game per-piece sprites live in assets/equipment/.

Rogue

Layered leathers. Near-black hide, strapped and buckled, matched daggers.

Starseer

Astral vestments. Violet-black cloth, crooked hat, star-tipped staff.
