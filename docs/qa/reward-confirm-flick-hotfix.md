# Reward confirmation and card flick hotfix

Base: `dev` at `6398f00e`. Feature branch: `feature/reward-confirm-flick-hotfix`.

## Behavior

- First touch selects a reward and enables Confirm without collecting. The inspection handler previously painted selection while consuming the touch event before the reward listener could run.
- Back retains selection. Confirm persists one selected card; refused or throwing saves restore the deck/checkpoint and expose retry feedback.
- Card drag recognition uses the shared 12 CSS-pixel tolerance. Normal play distance is 48 CSS pixels; Short/Long are 32/72. Optional quick flick uses at least 24 CSS pixels and 0.3 CSS pixels/ms upward velocity over a 100 ms window.
- Direct pointer drops remain. Short gestures do not require artwork overlap. Multiple enemies require an unambiguous directional target; returning toward the hand, cancellation, capture loss, blur, invalid targets and overlays cannot commit.
- Advanced → Gameplay stores Card drag distance and Card flick to play. Defaults apply to existing profiles without save migration.
- Component catalog: [updated card behavior](../COMPONENT-CATALOG.md).

## Threshold rationale

[Hammer pan](https://hammerjs.github.io/recognizer-pan/) uses a 10 px recognition threshold; [Hammer swipe](https://hammerjs.github.io/recognizer-swipe/) adds a 0.3 px/ms velocity threshold. [Android gesture guidance](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/touch-and-input/gestures/viewgroup) separates touch tolerance and fling velocity. These support separating gesture recognition from activation; the 24/48 px play distances are AshenSpire tuning choices, not an industry standard.
