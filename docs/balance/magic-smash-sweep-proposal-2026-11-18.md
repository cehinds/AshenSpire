These are built from existing rules only:
- **Smash:** a physical Smash deals +50% Poise damage for +1 cost. A **magic Smash deals +100% Poise damage** (owner: magic damage hits Ward first, so a magic Smash is the Ward and Poise breaker). Smash stance interplay applies.
- **Sweep:** hits every enemy for 30% less damage.
- **Cost curve:** 1 MP deals 5–8, 2 MP deals 15–20, 3 MP deals 25–30.
