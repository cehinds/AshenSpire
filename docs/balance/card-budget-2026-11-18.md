# Core card budget survey, 2026-11-18

Survey only: no gameplay code changed. It gives the numbers a re-tune of every Core card to a cost curve needs. Everything in the generated block (sections a to c) is reproduced by `node tools/card-budget-report.mjs --out docs/balance/card-budget-2026-11-18.md`; sections d to f are hand-written findings and survive a regeneration.

Method and caveats:

- Setup follows `tests/side-damage-scale-2026-11-18.test.mjs`: shipped Core profile, `createRunState` per class at level 1 with its baseline kit, `createRunCombat` against the Wandering Soldier, `previewCard`, then the same card played in a fresh identical fight.
- Neutral fight: soldier weaknesses cleared, 5000 HP, 1000 Poise pool (nothing clamps or breaks), player topped up so every card is affordable (costs are read before that). Class relics are removed for card rows: Forsaken Medallion adds +4 Poise damage to the first attack of a combat, and the enemy table keeps them.
- "Final damage" is the card-face number: after base scale x0.5, AR/PR adds, the card's own stance multiplier and the x0.7 player scale, before the target-side stance advantage and Poise protection. "HP vs soldier" is the HP the Poise-protected, Defend-stance soldier actually loses.
- Which cards a class holds: every colorless Core card, the class's own Core cards, plus the non-Core cards in its baseline starting deck (weapon arts: `greatswordSunderingHew`, `twinFang`, `blightTouch`; `base.ethereal`, `base.exhaust`). Only the baseline kit is measured; alternate kits (greatsword vs sword and shield, shortbow...) change the weapon arts and nothing else here.
- Category for the summary is primary-only: spell (camp:spell), else reflect, counter, evade, then the maneuver tag. Medians are across cards with a nonzero value in that cell.
- Ward from spells (`moteShield`) is a status (+5 Poise per stack, `fixCoreMechanics.json` `ward.enabled: false`), so it does not show in the Block or ward columns.

## (d) Reflect and drawback findings

### Reflect, as it stands

Code: `src/engine/guardStacks.js` (`reflectStackCount`, `gainReflectStacks`, `spendReflectStacks`, `reflectReducePct`, `reflectReturnPct`, `reflectCut`, `reflectReturned`), applied in `src/engine/gameProfileCombat.js` (the cut, ~line 244) and `src/engine/actions.js` (`reflectBack`, the return, ~line 481). Numbers: `content/framework/mechanics.json` `reflect`.

- Reflect is an integer on the combat entity (`reflectStacks`), not a status. It clears only when the fight ends. There is no `reflect` entry in `statuses.json` and no Reflect keyword; the only content references are the `applyReflect` op and the `trait:unreflectable` tag.
- One stack today: cuts the incoming hit by 30% (`reducePerStack` 0.3) before Block, and the landed hit then sends back 30% of the original, pre-cut hit (`returnPerStack` 0.3). Both are capped at 90% (`reduceCap` 0.9), reached at 3 stacks. `reflectCut = floor(incoming x (100 - cut%) / 100)`; `reflectReturned = floor(original x return% / 100)`.
- Every eligible hit that lands spends exactly 1 stack, whatever its size (so 3 stacks absorb three hits, the first at 90% cut and 90% return, the next at 60%, then 30%).
- Eligible hits come from another body and are not Counter or Reflect returns (`reflectedHit` / `counterReturnAttack` flags) and not `trait:unreflectable`. Evaded hits are not cut.
- Cap: `mechanics.reflect.stackCap` 10, but the shipped Core sets `fixCoreMechanics.json` `statusCaps: "none"`, so the only limit is the save-safety ceiling (`STACK_CEILING`). The 90% fraction cap stays.
- The return is the same damage type, flagged so it cannot reflect or counter again, and it is not scaled by the player/enemy side scale (it is final damage).
- Who grants it in Core: one card, `core.mirrorGuard` (colorless uncommon spell, 1 SP, 1 MP at the preview, `applyReflect` 1; upgraded 2). `base.reflect` exists in the non-Core pool.
- Combinations: no Core card combines Counter, Evade and Reflect stacks. `core.counter` grants Counter stacks (+ block), `core.evade`, `core.closingDodge`, `core.backstep` grant Evade (backstep also grants Prepared and exhausts), `core.mirrorGuard` grants Reflect. Only the Rogue's starting deck holds both a Counter and an Evade card (`core.counter` and `core.evade`).
- Counter stacks for comparison (`fixCoreMechanics.json` `counter`): `stacksByCard` (1 for `core.counter`), `returnPerStack` 0.1 of the hit, `consumePerHit` 1, `decayPerTurn` 1 (`mechanics.json` says 2, the FixCore value wins in Core), `stacksMax` 3 per grant. Evade: 3 Evasion per stack (`evadeStackBase`), lose 1 stack per round.

### Drained / attribute loss

There is no Drained tag, status or keyword: a case-insensitive search of `statuses.json`, `keywords.json`, `tagFamilies.csv`, `tagging.csv` and the core pack finds none (the only "drain" in Core is Poise drain, `fixCoreMechanics.json` `poise.drain: prevented`). The nearest things are Chill (-5% damage dealt per stack, and 1 less Stamina per 3 stacks), Shock (action loss chance), Silenced (spell damage x0.5), `weak`/`frail`. If a Drained effect is wanted it would be new content.

### Drawbacks that exist on Core cards (all 95 `core.*` cards)

- Exhaust keyword: `core.retreat` (also ethereal), `core.backstep`, `core.stunningBlow`, `core.whetstoneNick`, `core.temperedEdge`, `core.mendingTouch`, `core.steadyHands`, `core.healingPrayer`, `core.openArchive`. Ethereal: `core.retreat` only (plus `base.ethereal` kit cards).
- Self HP loss (`loseHp`, usually `nonlethal`): `core.meteor`, `core.pyreLash`, `core.forkedBolt`, `core.glacialSpike`, `core.radiantVerdict`, `core.witherDraught`, `core.batteringGale`. This is the MP-0 trade: the 0-SP/MP-1 or 2 spells pay HP.
- Cost increase per use: none. Every Core card is `costRule: "fixed"`. The one `ramp` is the opposite (a buff): `core.whetstoneNick` gets +1 damage per play for the run (`ramp: { scope: "run", stat: "damage", per: 1 }`), and it exhausts.
- Self Poise damage or break: none in Core cards. Self statuses applied are all beneficial (evade, prepared, regen, barrier, ward).
- Preview vs play differences found while measuring (report, not fixed): on smash cards with an explicit `poiseDamage` row (`hammerBlow`, `rendingSmash`, `stunningBlow`, `skullsplitter`, `ironbreaker`, and the Greatsword art) the preview's Poise number is x1.5 of what the play removes (the smash `poiseDamageBonus` 0.5 is previewed on the explicit row but not played). For the multi-hit `twinFang` the preview's `HP vs soldier` counts one hit where the play lands both. Counter's damage is a return dealt later, so its play removes nothing.
