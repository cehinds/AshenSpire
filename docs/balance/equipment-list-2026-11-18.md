# Equipment list, 2026-11-18 (proposal for owner approval)

How each item reads: **type base** (owner ruling §18) **+ one second bonus of its own**, so two items of the same type never play alike. ★ marks a new item; new items use placeholder art until real art exists. Bonuses are small, so totals stay inside the card cost curve. Owner item rulings from §16–17 are kept as written.
