# AshenSpire — Lore Bible

The one-line premise, expanded into a world the game's existing content already
points at. Everything here is consistent with `docs/GDD.md` §4, the enemy roster,
the event pool, the relic list and the four classes. Nothing here is required
reading for a rule (GDD §4.3): it is the ground the content stands on, not a
script the player must follow.

**Premise, one breath.** You are a Forsaken. The Goldbough — the living tree
whose light was the last kingdom — burned, and its light collapsed inward into a
tower of ash. Three great flames once kept that light: three hearths, one per
tier of the Spire. All three are cold. Only the corrupted still carry heat, in
the cinders that burn inside them. You climb, you kill, you take the cinders,
and with them you relight the flames — or you decide the old glory is not worth
the price and claim the crown for yourself.

---

## 1. The Goldbough and the Sovereign Ember

Before the Spire there was a tree. Not a tree in a kingdom; the tree **was** the
kingdom. The Goldbough grew from a single seed of starstone that fell in the
Fallow Marches, and its roots took the marsh, the foothills and the court
beneath its canopy. Its light did not come from the sun. It came from the
**Sovereign Ember** at its heart — a coal that never burned down, that gave heat
without ash.

The old civilization did not worship the Ember. They *used* it. Three flames
were lit from it, and the three flames ran the world:

| Flame | Hearth | What it kept | Tier of the Spire |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Field Flame** | the Bellfoundry, the marches' great bell tower | Growth. Harvest, healing, the seasons turning on time. | Act I — the Fallow Marches |
| **The Court Flame** | the throne hall of the Gilded Court | Order. Oaths held, law kept, the knight's word binding. | Act II — the Stitched Court |
| **The Crown Flame** | the Furnace Chapel beneath the canopy | Memory. The dead remembered, names kept, the Ember itself fed. | Act III — the Ashen Crown |

Each flame was tended by an order: **Wardens** for the field, **Knights** for
the court, **Saints** for the crown. Each order kept a horn, a bell, or a
censer. Several survive as relics (Warden Horn, Ancestral Horn, Howling
Standard, Radiant Aegis) and none of them work the way they used to.

## 2. The Burning

Nobody living agrees on why the Goldbough burned. The three tellings the player
meets on the road are all partly true:

- **The Warden's telling.** The Crown Flame was fed too much. The Saints, wanting
  the dead to never be forgotten, burned cinders in the Chapel until the Ember
  could not hold the heat, and the tree caught from the top down.
- **The Court's telling.** The Wardens let the Blight in. Rot in the roots,
  ignored for a season too long, until the trunk was hollow and the fire had
  nowhere to go but inward.
- **The Astronomer's telling.** The starstone seed was never meant to grow this
  large. The Goldbough was a fire that took a thousand years to fall. What
  happened was not a disaster. It was the seed finishing.

What is not disputed: the light collapsed *inward*. The canopy fell, the trunk
fused, and what remained was the **Ashen Spire** — a tower of vitrified ash
standing where the tree had stood, three tiers high, the Marches at its foot,
the Court in its belly, the Crown at its top. Ember still moves in it. Not down
from the heart, as it should, but *up*, thin and starving, toward the crown, as
if the Sovereign Ember is trying to leave.

## 3. Cinders, the Corrupted, and what "relight" means

When the flames went out the heat did not vanish. It went into whatever was
closest and would hold it. That is the **Blight**: not a plague, but ember
without a hearth, burning inside things that were never built to burn.

- A soldier who held the line at the Fell Courtyard is still holding it. He
  is a **Wandering Soldier** now, and there is a cinder where his heart was.
- The Gilded Court did not want to die, so it had its surgeons stitch it back
  together. The **Stitched King** is what a throne looks like when it refuses
  to be empty.
- The Saints of the Furnace Chapel kept feeding the Crown Flame long after the
  tree was gone. The **Furnace Saint** is the one who never stopped.

A **cinder** is the flame's heat in a form that can be carried. Every corrupted
thing you cut down leaves one, and cinders are the only currency in the Spire
because they are the only thing in the Spire that is still alive. The merchant
takes them. The smith burns them. The shrines drink them. And the three cold
hearths will take them too, if you bring enough.

**Relighting a flame** is therefore not a ritual. It is a butcher's ledger. To
put the Field Flame back in the Bellfoundry you have to take back the heat that
went into the Bell Keeper, the Thorn Matriarch, the Fell Warden and every
blight-hound between. The act boss holds the largest cinder on the tier; killing
it is what lights the hearth. This is the loop the player already runs — three
acts, three bosses, cinders spent on the way — given its name.

## 4. The Forsaken

A Forsaken is someone the Goldbough's light *left*. In the old kingdom every
soul was warmed by one of the three flames — a field-born, a court-born, a
crown-born — and was written into the Chapel's memory at death. The Forsaken
were never written. They were the ones outside the canopy when it fell: exiles,
the unnamed, the ones the light did not reach. That is why they can climb. The
Blight takes hold through the flame-mark, and the Forsaken never had one.

The Forsaken Medallion is a slug of cold iron, blank on both faces. It is the
only badge of the order, and it is a badge only in the sense that a gravestone
with no name is a badge.

A Forsaken has no *right* to the Ember. They are not restoring their own
kingdom. Every ending of the climb is a choice about what the glory of somebody
else's civilization is worth:

- **Restore.** Relight all three, return the Sovereign Ember to the heart, let
  the tree grow again — and its light will not reach you this time either.
- **Claim.** Take the Ember at the crown for yourself. Become the hearth.
- **Transform.** Let it out. The ember has been climbing for a reason.

The game does not tell the player which is correct. The Hollow Astronomer, the
Furnace Saint and the Blighted Valkyrie are each the champion of one answer.

## 5. The four who climb

The classes are not professions. They are four different relationships with the
fire.

- **Reaver** — *blood and steel.* A marches-born mercenary who never took the
  Warden's oath. Fights in two stances because the Wardens taught two: one to
  break, one to hold. Bleed is the Reaver's argument that a thing with a cinder
  in it still has blood to lose. Bloodflame Stance is the closest a Forsaken can
  come to carrying a flame: it burns the wielder first.
- **Rogue** — *ash and opportunism.* A scavenger of the Stitched Court, one of
  the people the court stitched over. Learned poison from the Court Surgeons and
  speed from the Marionettes. Does not believe in the flames and does not need
  to; a clean opening is a clean opening.
- **Starseer** — *starstone.* An apprentice of the Eclipse Observatory who read
  the Astronomer's charts and left before the eclipse. Casts in sequence because
  starstone answers rhythm, not force; the second spell is always the true one.
  Fragile because starstone remembers what it was before it was a tree.
- **Herald** — *gold and rot.* The last novice of the Furnace Chapel. Spends
  their own health to act because that is what a Saint does: they feed the
  flame from themselves. Their rot is the Blight, turned outward on purpose.
  The Herald is the only climber who actually wants the flames back, and the
  one the flames would cost the most.

## 6. The three tiers

### Act I — The Fallow Marches (the Field Flame)

The foothills at the Spire's base, where the roots were. Umber, iron, faint
gold. Growth without a Field Flame does not stop; it goes wrong. Briar Hermits
root themselves to stay out of the wind. Lantern Moths carry sparks of ember
dust looking for a hearth. The Goldbough Avatar, grown into a golden sapling,
still holds out a hand — it does not know the tree is gone.

- **The Fell Warden** kept the Fell Courtyard gate. He still does. He fights
  with a cane because his leg went the way of the marches.
- **The Bell Keeper** rang the Bellfoundry every dawn so the fields knew the
  hour. The bell is cracked and the dawn does not come, and he rings it anyway.
- **The Thorn Matriarch** was the Wardens' last planting: a warden grafted to
  briar to hold the Sanctum after the order failed. She held.

The Grave of the Nameless is on this tier: a cairn of broken swords, the graves
of Forsaken who climbed before you and were never written into memory. What the
player does there follows them up the Spire.

### Act II — The Stitched Court (the Court Flame)

The Spire's belly, where the trunk was. Gilded stone, oxidized metal,
surgical joins, verdigris. When the Court Flame went out every oath in the
kingdom came due at once, and the Court answered the only way it could: it had
its surgeons stitch every knight, hound and courtier back to their post. Order,
kept by force, past the point of meaning anything.

- **The Stitched King** has a thousand hands because everyone who swore to him
  is still sewn to the throne.
- **The Glass Regent** ruled in the Hall of Mirrors while the King was
  "indisposed." She is made entirely of the court's reflections of itself, and
  she shatters the same way.
- **The Marrow Organist** plays the Ossuary Organ Hall. The pipes are the
  court's dead. He is the only one in the Court who still remembers the
  funeral service, and he is playing it for all of them at once.

### Act III — The Ashen Crown (the Crown Flame)

The top of the Spire, where the canopy burned. Mineral ash, bone light,
concentrated ember. Here the starving ember pools, and here the things it is
pooling *toward* wait. Ash Revenants reform because the Crown Flame's job was
memory, and it is still trying to remember them into shape. Ember-Starved
Pilgrims came up hoping to be warmed.

- **The Furnace Saint** is the keeper of the Crown Flame's hearth in the
  Furnace Chapel. He is still feeding it. Beat him and the hearth is yours.
- **The Hollow Astronomer** watched the fall from the Eclipse Observatory and
  concluded the tree was always going to burn. He wants the Ember to *leave*.
  He will call the heavens down to see it off.
- **The Ashheart Dragon** sleeps in the Ashheart Caldera around the last of the
  root-heat, the wyrm the Wyrm Aspirants and Wyrm Lords were consecrated to.
  It is the Goldbough's oldest tenant and does not care what you came for.
- **The Blighted Valkyrie** waits at the Crown itself. She was the last soul
  written into the Chapel's memory before it burned — the last one the light
  saved — and the Blight took her hardest of all. She is what *restore* looks
  like from the inside.

## 7. Vocabulary for authors

Use these words consistently in card text, event copy, tooltips and item
descriptions.

| Word | Means | Never means |
|---|---|---|
| **Ember** | the living heat of the Goldbough; the thing that flows up the Spire | fire in general, a status effect |
| **Cinder** | ember in a carried, spendable form; the run's currency; what the corrupted leave | a coin, a small fire |
| **Flame / hearth** | one of the three great fires and its seat; the act's goal | any campfire or rest node |
| **Blight** | ember without a hearth, burning inside a host; the corruption | disease, poison (the Rogue's poison is not Blight) |
| **Forsaken** | one the light never marked; the player, and those who climbed before | a class name, an insult |
| **Nameless** | Forsaken who died on the climb and were never written into memory | generic undead |
| **Goldbough** | the tree-kingdom, past tense | the Spire, the current world |
| **Starstone** | the seed the tree grew from; the Starseer's element | generic mana or magic |
| **Written / unwritten** | remembered by the Crown Flame or not | literally literate |

Tone: short, concrete, physical. A sentence about the world should name a
thing you could pick up. Avoid "ancient," "eldritch," "eternal." Prefer *cold,
cracked, stitched, starving, still ringing*.

## 8. What stays open

Deliberately unanswered, so events and quest chains have room:

- Who lit the first flame from the Ember, and whether they were Forsaken too.
- Whether the Ember is fleeing the Spire or being *called* up by something above
  the Crown.
- What the Keeper of the Nameless is walking toward, and whether the second
  cairn is an ending or a door.
- Whether a relit flame stays lit after the climber leaves.
