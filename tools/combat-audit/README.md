This audit found and fixed the primary Quit menu action and two measured initialization costs: the Settings card sample now renders when its folded Preview is opened, and card value projection groups authored tags once per operation. The projection change preserves independent pre-change value hashes. No gameplay defect was confirmed by the card matrix. The unrelated Ward-to-Block/stance implementation merged from Dev is preserved and the current proofs below include it.

Every accepted matrix play checks legal targets, paid and resolved receipts, actual payment/restoration in each resource pool, bounded prompts/queues, end turn, next turn and a subsequent play. Immediate positive numeric damage, Block and healing require operation receipts; counter cards must arm their deferred payload. Refusal snapshots include enemies, piles, turn/phase, trigger gates, prompts, queued actions and RNG counters, excluding only derived preview caches. The 33 empty-pool accepted plays have zero effective prices; they are not mislabeled as self-funding simply because they contain a restoration opcode.

Conditional proofs use the actual legal target, controlled predicate states, attributed operation packets and actual HP/Block/Barrier/status/pile changes. Same-op coeffects count only when their other predicates remain stable; an amount receipt alone cannot establish a changing complementary branch. The general and focused helpers, deferred proofs and five exact-state complementary family closures establish 42 of 43 literal authored condition shapes. The remaining frost/frostExposed shape is projected to frozen/chilled and is proven through resolved dispatch, giving representative positive/negative outcome coverage for all 43 semantic families. The family-closure helper independently models authored branch amounts plus stance entry, recovery, skill hooks and status multipliers; it does not sum emitted receipts as its oracle. These representative closures do not turn the 106 still-uncovered individual indices into passes.

Real Guard Counter and Crimson Reprisal fixtures verify authored preparation, no premature HP damage, eligible incoming attack, exact conditional HP/Poise payload, the implemented stance multipliers, suppression/bypass, declined reaction, expiration and input recovery. The four lifecycle scenarios and twelve conditional cases run under both loaders. Enemy reactions remain enabled.
