# Necrotic (`damage:decay`)
Applies Blight on a Health hit (Crimson in classic combat; Necrotic in expanded combat) unless the card already prints a Crimson Blight effect.
- Carried by: cards.
- Lives in: `src/engine/combatRounds/resolver.js` (`resolveRound`).
- Portability: legacy (service.json is empty until the behaviour moves to catalog ops).
