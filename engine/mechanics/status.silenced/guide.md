# Silenced (`status.silenced`)
Spells cost 1 more Stamina per stack. Immune to Force: each Force hit or effect is stopped and removes 1 stack.
- Carried by: statuses on any entity.
- Lives in: `src/engine/actions.js` (`spendSilencedStack`).
- Portability: legacy (service.json is empty until the behaviour moves to catalog ops).
