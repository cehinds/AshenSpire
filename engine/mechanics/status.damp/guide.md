# Damp (`status.damp`)
Lightning against it rolls with advantage to Shock. Burn applied to it is halved. Any Fire damage dries it. 1 stack expires each turn.
- Carried by: statuses on any entity.
- Lives in: `src/engine/elementLightning.js` (`fireDriesDamp`).
- Portability: legacy (service.json is empty until the behaviour moves to catalog ops).
