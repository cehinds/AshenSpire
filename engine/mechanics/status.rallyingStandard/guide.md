# Rallying Standard (`status.rallyingStandard`)
At the start of your turn: gain 1 Strength and take 1 damage (per stack).
- Carried by: statuses on any entity.
- Lives in: `src/content/statuses.js`.
- Defined in: `content/source/statuses.json`.
- Portability: legacy (service.json is empty until the behaviour moves to catalog ops).
