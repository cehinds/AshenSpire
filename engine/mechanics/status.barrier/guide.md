# Barrier (`status.barrier`)
Each stack gives +5 maximum and current Poise. Lose 1 stack each turn, and its +5 maximum Poise with it.
- Carried by: statuses on any entity.
- Lives in: `src/engine/actions.js` (`gainBarrierAmount`).
- Portability: legacy (service.json is empty until the behaviour moves to catalog ops).
