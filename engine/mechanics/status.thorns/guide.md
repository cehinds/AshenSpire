# Thorns (`status.thorns`)
An attacker that lands a hit on the holder takes damage equal to its Thorns stacks. Constant: it does not decay; only a timed effect, printed on its card, changes it.
- Carried by: statuses on any entity.
- Lives in: `src/engine/actions.js` (`ThornsReturn`).
- Portability: legacy (service.json is empty until the behaviour moves to catalog ops).
