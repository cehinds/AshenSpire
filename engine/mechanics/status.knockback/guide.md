# Knockback (`status.knockback`)
Knocked back by a fight saved before Force lost its push: it must Advance (pay its Advance cost) before its next melee move. New fights never apply it.
- Carried by: statuses on any entity.
- Lives in: `src/engine/elementIdentity.js` (`OnPoiseBreak`).
- Portability: legacy (service.json is empty until the behaviour moves to catalog ops).
