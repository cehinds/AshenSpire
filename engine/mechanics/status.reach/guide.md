# Reach (`status.reach`)
The holder's physical attacks have Reach: they hit a back-row or Retreat-stacked enemy without removing its Retreat stacks. 1 stack expires at the end of its owner's turn.
- Carried by: statuses on any entity.
- Lives in: `src/engine/bandRows.js` (`DefaultEnemyRow`).
- Portability: legacy (service.json is empty until the behaviour moves to catalog ops).
