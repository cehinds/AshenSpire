# Smash (`maneuver:smash`)
Heavy strike. Deals extra damage against physical Guard and extra Poise damage when Guard breaks. Fast Attack and Counter answer it. Stance benefit: +50% Poise damage.
- Carried by: cards.
- Lives in: `src/engine/gearReceipt.js` (`GearMeleeHit`).
- Portability: legacy (service.json is empty until the behaviour moves to catalog ops).
