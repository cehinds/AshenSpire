# Sweep (`maneuver:sweep`)
Windmill strike. Bypasses melee Counter; physical Guard and ranged attacks answer it. Stance benefit: an attack also hits every other enemy in range for 50% of its damage.
- Carried by: cards.
- Lives in: `src/engine/combatReactions.js` (`eligible`).
- Portability: legacy (service.json is empty until the behaviour moves to catalog ops).
