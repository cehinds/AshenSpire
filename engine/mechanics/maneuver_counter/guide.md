# Counter (`maneuver:counter`)
Prepare one reaction. Reduce damage from one eligible hit. A fully absorbed eligible hit triggers a stronger reply; Sweep, Force and Piercing bypass melee Counter. Stance benefit: +25% Block gained; a hit that costs you HP returns 25% of that HP to the attacker.
- Carried by: cards.
- Lives in: `src/engine/combatReactions.js` (`counterCoversAttack`).
- Portability: legacy (service.json is empty until the behaviour moves to catalog ops).
