# Magic Focus (`item:magic-focus`)
What this piece IS: the Armoury and the smith name it Magic Focus. While in Prepared stance, an equipped Focus lowers the final Mana cost of each spell by 1 (never below 0). Only one Focus discount applies.
- Carried by: gears.
- Lives in: `src/engine/skillXp.js`.
- Portability: legacy (service.json is empty until the behaviour moves to catalog ops).
