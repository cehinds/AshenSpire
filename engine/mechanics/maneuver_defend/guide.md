# Defend (`maneuver:defend`)
Prepare defense. Physical cards protect Guard; defensive spell cards protect Ward. Smash breaks physical Guard. Stance benefit: +50% Block gained and 25% less Poise damage taken.
- Carried by: cards.
- Lives in: `src/model/actionCosts.js` (`actionStance`).
- Portability: legacy (service.json is empty until the behaviour moves to catalog ops).
