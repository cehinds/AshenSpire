Your pose workshop

  1. Choose a starter from Open, or import your own sprites.
  2. Drag poses into the timeline. Repeat, replace or reorder them.
  3. Adjust timing and alignment in Frame properties. Use onion skin to compare frames.
  4. Press Play sequence to preview your order.
  5. Save sequence keeps a named local copy; Export makes a portable backup.

Space plays or pauses. Ctrl+S saves. Ctrl+Z undoes; Ctrl+Shift+Z redoes. These shortcuts leave text and number fields alone.

Browser and desktop saves are separate. Transfer sequences with JSON. This workshop does not modify the game or generate new artwork automatically.
