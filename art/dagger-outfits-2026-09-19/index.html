Legal combinations

Dagger + Parrying Dagger is a legal dual-blade pairing because both share the blade type. Two copies of the same Dagger cannot be equipped: placing it in another hand moves it. A two-handed weapon requires the other hand empty. Other one-handed pairings remain subject to the existing stat and ownership rules.
